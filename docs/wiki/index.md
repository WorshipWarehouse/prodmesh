Landing page for the prodmesh wiki: what it is, and where to go next.

# prodmesh

prodmesh is a LAN-hosted production dashboard for churches. One always-on box
runs the server; every other screen in the building — room Macs, booth
displays, tablets — is just a browser pointed at it. It controls room modes
through Bitfocus Companion, shows service plans from Planning Center, tracks
Run of Show live against ProPresenter, and measures loudness via Smaart or
ProdMesh Remote RTA.

It's built for church tech volunteers and part-time staff — people comfortable
with AV gear, not necessarily with servers or Docker.
